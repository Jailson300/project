About Us

Preserving the Legacy of the Meitei Civilization

The Meitei people, the indigenous inhabitants of Kangleipak (present-day Manipur), possess a rich and vibrant heritage shaped by centuries of history, culture, tradition, and innovation. From ancient kingdoms and sacred traditions to classical dance, indigenous sports, and a unique writing system, the Meitei civilization represents one of South Asia's most remarkable cultural legacies.

This platform was created to celebrate, preserve, and share that legacy with the world.

Our Purpose

The purpose of this website is to preserve, celebrate, and share the rich heritage of the Meitei people with audiences around the world. As one of the oldest indigenous communities of Northeast India, the Meitei community possesses a unique history, language, literature, traditions, arts, and cultural practices that deserve to be documented and appreciated by future generations.

This platform serves as a reliable source of information, bringing together the origins, historical milestones, customs, festivals, traditional attire, cuisine, architecture, sports, and contributions of the Meitei people in one place. By presenting this knowledge through an engaging and accessible digital experience, we aim to make Meitei heritage easier to explore for students, researchers, travelers, and anyone interested in learning about the community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a digital archive that preserves and promotes:

  • Ancient History and Origins
  • Cultural Practices and Traditions
  • Festivals and Ritual
  • Meitei Mayek Script and Language
  • Art, dance, and music
  • Historical monuments and heritage sites
  • Sports and martial traditions
  • Contributions of the Meitei people to society

    • A journey Through Time

    For centuries, the land of Kangleipak flourished as an independent kingdom with its own governance, literature, architecture, and social institutions. Historical chronicles, ancient manuscripts, and oral traditions continue to preserve the stories of kings, warriors, scholars, artists, and ordinary people who shaped the identity of the Meitei civilization.

    The heritage of the Meitei people is not merely a record of the past—it is a living tradition that continues to evolve while remaining deeply connected to its roots.

    Culture That Lives Through Generations

    The heart of Meitei identity lies in its culture

    From the graceful performances of Ras Leela to the vibrant celebrations of Yaoshang and Lai Haraoba, every tradition reflects a deep connection between community, spirituality, and nature.

    The preservation of traditional arts, handloom weaving, indigenous beliefs, and oral literature demonstrates the resilience and creativity of a people determined to keep their heritage alive.

    The Birthplace of Modern Polo

    One of the most celebrated contributions of the Meitei people to the world is Sagol Kangjei, the traditional horseback game recognized as the origin of modern polo.

    Beyond polo, the Meitei community has a long-standing sporting tradition reflected in indigenous games such as Mukna, Mukna Kangjei, and Hiyang Tannaba, showcasing strength, discipline, teamwork, and cultural pride.

    Preserving Heritage for Future Generations

    Heritage is more than monuments and historical records—it is the collective memory of a people.

    By documenting the stories, traditions, achievements, and values of the Meitei civilization, we hope to create a lasting resource for students, researchers, educators, travelers, and future generations.

    Every page on this platform is a step toward preserving the identity and legacy of the Meitei people for years to come.
