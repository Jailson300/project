LAI HARAOBA

Lai Haraoba is one of the oldest and most significant traditional festivals of the Meitei people. Meaning “merry-making of the gods,” it is a vibrant celebration honoring the Lai (traditional deities) through sacred rituals, music, dance, songs, and storytelling. The festival portrays the creation of the world, human life, and the traditional way of living, passing ancient knowledge and cultural values from one generation to the next.

YAOSHANG

Yaoshang is one of Manipur's most vibrant festivals, celebrated for five days with traditional rituals, music, dance, sports, and community gatherings. Rooted in Meitei tradition, it beautifully blends spirituality, youthful energy, and cultural celebration, bringing communities together in a joyful expression of heritage.

CHEIRAOBA

Cheiraoba is the traditional Meitei New Year festival, celebrated with prayers, family gatherings, traditional food, and the symbolic climbing of hills. It marks a fresh beginning, carrying hopes for prosperity, good health, and happiness in the year ahead.

NINGOL CHAKOUBA

Ningol Chakouba is a cherished Meitei festival that celebrates the special bond between sisters and their brothers. Through family gatherings, traditional feasts, gifts, and heartfelt reunions, the festival strengthens love, respect, and the enduring ties of family.

HEIKRU HIDONGBA

Heikru Hidongba is a traditional Meitei boat-racing festival held at the sacred Ibudhou Pakhang Laiseng in Manipur, bringing communities together through spirited competition, prayers, music, and celebration. It symbolizes unity, harmony, and the enduring cultural heritage of the Meitei people.

KANG YATRA

Kang Yatra is a traditional Meitei festival dedicated to Lord Jagannath, celebrated with devotional rituals, processions, music, and the traditional game of Kang. It beautifully brings together faith, community, and the living cultural heritage of Manipur.
