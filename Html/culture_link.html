TRADITIONAL INSTRUMENT :

Pena

The pena is a traditional, mono-stringed bowed musical instrument from Manipur, India, deeply embedded in Meitei culture and folklore.

Learn more

Pung

Pung is a traditional Meitei drum and an essential percussion instrument in Manipuri music, dance, and religious ceremonies.

Learn more

Dhol

Dhol is a traditional double-headed drum used in Meitei and Manipuri musical traditions, providing strong rhythmic accompaniment for folk performances, dances, festivals, and community celebrations.

Learn more

Bansi(Flute)

Bansi is a traditional bamboo flute used in Meitei and Manipuri music, known for its gentle melodies and role in folk performances, devotional music, and cultural celebrations.

Pheijom, Lengyan & Kurta

The Pride of Meitei Men

A timeless ensemble of Meitei men, where Pheijom, Kurta, and Lengyan come together to embody dignity, elegance, and cultural pride.. Kurta is the upper tunic, Pheijom is the traditional lower garment, and Lengyan is a shawl worn over the Kurta, especially during weddings, festivals, and formal ceremonies.
